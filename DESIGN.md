# Backpressure Lab visual system

The product has two connected surfaces in one restrained visual world. The root route is a **field-guide landing page**: charcoal canvas, warm ink, one amber action color, generous editorial spacing, an annotated pipeline figure, and scenario rows that make the mechanism understandable before operation. `/lab` is the **operations runroom**: the same quiet materials become denser around a sticky control rail, live pipeline, metric strip, and signal plots.

Typography uses a restrained sans-serif UI voice for explanation and a monospace measurement voice for rates, IDs, states, and chart labels. Surfaces are flat and structured with thin rules, small radii, and no decorative gradients or shadows. A desaturated sage signal indicates healthy processing; red is reserved for overload/failure.

The landing page leads to one clear action: open the lab with the traffic-spike scenario preselected. The lab keeps controls, current state, and telemetry in one focused working layout. Both surfaces remain responsive without horizontal scrolling, and motion is limited to live progress changes and smooth anchor navigation with `prefers-reduced-motion` support.

The memorable moment is still the operator reading under the pipeline: a short explanation changes from healthy to degrading to overloaded while the live queue and tail latency show why.
