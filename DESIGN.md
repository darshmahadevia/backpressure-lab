# Backpressure Lab visual system

The product is a **restrained operations runroom**: a quiet dark landing page that leads into a precise instrument for watching finite capacity become visible. The canvas is charcoal and warm off-white, with one amber accent for action and pressure, a desaturated sage signal for healthy processing, and red reserved for overload/failure. The pipeline is the hero; charts support the mechanism instead of replacing it.

Typography uses a restrained sans-serif UI voice for explanation and a monospace measurement voice for rates, IDs, states, and chart labels. Surfaces are flat and structured with thin rules, small radii, and no decorative gradients or shadows. The landing page uses generous editorial spacing and proof-like content; the experiment surface becomes denser only when the user starts operating it.

The responsive composition keeps the headline and primary run action first, then stacks the pipeline, metrics, and signal plots without horizontal scrolling. Motion is limited to live progress changes and smooth anchor navigation, and respects `prefers-reduced-motion`; the data is readable in its settled state.

The page's memorable moment is the operator reading under the pipeline: a short explanation changes from healthy to degrading to overloaded while the live queue and tail latency show why.
