# Backpressure Lab visual system

The product has two connected surfaces in one restrained visual world. The root route is a **field-guide landing page**: charcoal canvas, warm ink, one amber action color, generous editorial spacing, an annotated pipeline figure, and scenario rows that make the mechanism understandable before operation. `/lab` is the **operations runroom**: the same quiet materials become denser around a sticky control rail, live pipeline, metric strip, and signal plots.

Typography uses a loaded IBM Plex Sans variable family for explanation and interface hierarchy, paired with IBM Plex Mono for rates, IDs, states, and chart labels. Weights stay deliberate: regular body copy, medium headings, and semibold actions. Surfaces are flat and structured with thin rules, small radii, and no decorative gradients or shadows. A desaturated sage signal indicates healthy processing; red is reserved for overload/failure.

Both surfaces support dark and light modes through the same semantic tokens, with dark as the default and the visitor's preference persisted. The landing page has desktop anchors plus a real mobile menu; the lab has a clear Overview breadcrumb and a cross-platform custom scenario listbox rather than relying on browser-specific native select styling. The landing page leads to one clear action: open the lab with the traffic-spike scenario preselected. The lab keeps controls, current state, and telemetry in one focused working layout.

Motion is restrained and causal: a short first-load reveal, menu/dropdown feedback, smooth anchor continuity, and live progress transitions. All are disabled or reduced under `prefers-reduced-motion`. The memorable moment is still the operator reading under the pipeline: a short explanation changes from healthy to degrading to overloaded while the live queue and tail latency show why.
